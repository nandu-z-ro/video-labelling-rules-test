# Video labelling rules

Every clip gets exactly one label: **keep**, **age_restrict** or **remove**.
Each label comes with a reason and the timestamp of the moment that decided it.

## keep
- No graphic violence, gore, self-harm or sexual content.
- News, commentary, education and documentary clips with no graphic moments.
- Brief, non-graphic mentions of violence (a reporter describing events).

## age_restrict
- Real news or documentary footage that shows graphic violence, injury or death,
  where the footage has clear news or public-interest value.
- Protest, war and disaster footage from news outlets or eyewitnesses that is
  graphic but informs viewers about real events.
- Educational content (medical, historical) with graphic images.

## remove
- Graphic violence, gore or cruelty with no news, educational or documentary value.
- Content that glorifies, encourages or instructs violence or self-harm.
- Shock compilations, fight videos made for entertainment, and clips aimed at teens
  that show harmful acts.

## News exception
Graphic real news is **age-restricted, not removed**, when all three hold:
1. It shows a real event (protest, war, disaster, crime) and is not staged.
2. The framing informs (news report, eyewitness record, documentary), not celebrates.
3. The graphic moment is part of the story, not the point of the clip.

If any one fails, judge the clip on the normal rules above.
Example: Ana's protest footage shows injuries during a real protest, filmed as an
eyewitness record, so it is age-restricted, not removed.

## Evidence
For every label, give the timestamp (mm:ss) of the deciding moment and one line on why.
